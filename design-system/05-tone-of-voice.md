# Tone of Voice & Writing — Cash Back Moto

The brand voice is **confident, direct, and energetic** — like a salesperson who knows the product inside out and doesn't waste your time. No fluff. No corporate speak.

---

## Brand Personality

| Trait | What it means in copy |
|---|---|
| **Confident** | State facts, don't hedge. "The best scooters in Egypt" not "We try to offer good scooters." |
| **Direct** | Get to the point in the first sentence. No long-winded intros. |
| **Energetic** | Short sentences. Active verbs. Momentum. |
| **Approachable** | Not cold or stiff. Talk like a human, not a brochure. |
| **Local** | Egyptian audience. Acknowledge that. Don't write like a global brand ignoring context. |

---

## English Voice

### Do
- Short sentences. Power words. Action verbs.
- Write headlines in **ALL CAPS** for hero and section titles.
- Use "you" and "your" — talk directly to the reader.
- Lead with the benefit, not the feature.

```
✅ FIND YOUR RIDE
✅ Ask for the price — we'll get back to you fast.
✅ Gas or electric. New or classic. We have it all.
✅ Two locations. Alexandria & Cairo.
```

### Don't
```
❌ We are pleased to present our wide range of motorcycle solutions.
❌ Our company has been committed to providing quality vehicles.
❌ Please do not hesitate to contact us for further inquiries.
```

## Arabic Voice

Arabic copy should match the same energy — not a literal translation. Arabic has its own rhythm and idioms. Use them.

### Principles
- Use **Modern Standard Arabic (MSA)** with a slightly informal, friendly tone. Not dialect, not overly formal.
- Short, punchy sentences work in Arabic too — don't let the language balloon copy.
- Avoid passive voice where possible in Arabic (the active form is stronger).
- RTL layout means the **visual weight** of headlines reads right-to-left — short words on the right land harder. Keep that in mind.

```
✅ اختار دراجتك
✅ سكوتر أو موتوسيكل، بنزين أو كهربا — عندنا كل حاجة
✅ فرعين: الإسكندرية والقاهرة
✅ اسأل عن السعر — هنرد عليك في الحال
```

## CTA Copy

Every call-to-action should have **one clear action verb** and feel immediate.

| Context | English | Arabic |
|---|---|---|
| Product inquiry | Ask for Price | اسأل عن السعر |
| WhatsApp contact | Chat with Us | تواصل معنا |
| Browse catalog | Explore Models | اتفرج على الموديلات |
| Phone call | Call Now | اتصل دلوقتي |
| Location | Find Us | امتا تيجي؟ |
| Hero primary CTA | FIND YOUR RIDE | اختار دراجتك |

---

## What to Avoid

- **Superlatives without substance** — "The #1 motorcycle shop" → only use if provable.
- **Filler phrases** — "Feel free to", "Don't hesitate to", "We are proud to announce"
- **Passive voice** — "Motorcycles are sold here" → "We sell motorcycles."
- **Overly formal Arabic** — فصحى that sounds like a government document.
- **Emoji in headings** — allowed only in informal social-style sections or mobile CTAs, never in hero or nav.

## Page-by-Page Voice Notes

### Home
Punchy and fast. The hero headline should stop someone mid-scroll. Minimal body copy. Let the bikes do the talking.

### Catalog
Functional and clear. Filter labels, brand names, and model names — no marketing fluff here. Users are in browse mode.

### About
Slightly warmer. Tell the story briefly. Who are we, what do we sell, where are we. 3 short paragraphs max.

### Contact
Ultra direct. Phone number big and bold. WhatsApp button obvious. No paragraph needed — just the info.
